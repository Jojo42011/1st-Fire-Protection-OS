Schedule

Who is doing what, where, and when?
The week grid, proposal and unscheduled queue are real reads from the ServiceTrade schedule mirror, office-scoped. The metric strip shows the counts the mirror provides: techs available and cancellations are not tracked in the data yet, so the real figures the endpoint returns (scheduled today, unassigned, technicians out) are shown instead of inventing them. Workload bars count booked jobs, not hours, because appointment durations are not always present. Approving a slot commits the crew and starts reminders, so nothing sends without your approval.

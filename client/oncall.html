The after-hours receptionist reads this roster to know who to reach. Pick a specific office up top to manage its rotation.
The roster is a real read, office-scoped. It is single-cover: one person holds each week, so there is no simultaneous backup in the data. "Next up" is who relieves them in the rotation, and an office is flagged a gap when no one is on call now, a night in the next 14 days is uncovered, or the current person has no phone on file. After-hours call volume and unanswered counts are not in this roster data (they live on the Phones screen), so they are shown as not tracked here rather than guessed.

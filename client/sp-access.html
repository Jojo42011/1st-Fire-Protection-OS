Convert folders to group access

Put each office's shared folders behind one security group. Active staff move into the group, links and former staff are revoked.
Some groups still need creating in AD, and every group's membership is set in AD.
Your SharePoint groups sync up from on-prem AD, so both the groups and their membership are managed there, not in the cloud. This script creates the new groups and adds each group's "move in" people (resolved to their AD accounts). Run it on a domain controller, then force an AD Connect delta sync. Once synced, the rows turn convertible and Convert only grants the folders and clears the direct shares.

        
      

    

    
One row per security group. Only groups that exist on-prem (in AD) can convert; a cloud-only group (in Entra, not AD) must be migrated first with the card above. Move in = the people who should be group members; membership is set in AD (the script above), not by Convert, because a synced group's members are managed on-prem. Revoke = direct shares removed (org/anonymous links plus the individual grants now covered by the group): the active people among them keep access through the group, so this is not a count of people losing access. Keep = grants left for you to check by hand.

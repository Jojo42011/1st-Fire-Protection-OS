Pipeline

Where is open work in the funnel?
Open pipeline is the sum of lead, quoted and following-up stages from ServiceTrade. Weighted pipeline is an estimate: each open stage is discounted by an assumed close probability (lead 20%, quoted 50%, following up 70%), shown amber and dotted. It is a forecast, never booked revenue. Won and lost reflect the current board, not a period-to-date total, because this feed is not period-scoped. The office breakdown is the open-quote cut from ServiceTrade.

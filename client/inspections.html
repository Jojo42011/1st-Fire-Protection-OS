Inspections

Run an NFPA inspection (10 extinguishers, 25 sprinkler, 72 alarm), score every line, and print a letterheaded AHJ report. Failed items drop straight into the deficiencies backlog to be quoted.

Source: OS-native inspection records. NFPA intervals are reference guidance; the authority having jurisdiction governs. Not a substitute for the adopted edition of the standard.

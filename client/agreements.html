Service Agreements

What recurring work and renewal revenue needs attention?
Agreement book
Recurring agreements read from the ServiceTrade mirror, office-scoped. Agreement value is the contract price on file: it is not asserted as annualized revenue, and it is not booked revenue. "Renewing" and "at risk" windows are computed from each agreement's end date. Visits complete is not shown because the mirror does not carry visit progress yet, and "Owner" shows the owning office (there is no individual account owner in the data).

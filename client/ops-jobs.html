Readiness is a first-pass SOP: a completed job is ready to bill only when it carries the office/entity and a customer contact accounting needs (2 checks). A per-job dollar value is not shown because the ServiceTrade mirror does not carry job amounts yet, so the value column reads n/a rather than inventing a figure. "Owner" shows the owning office and on-file customer contact; there is no individual assignee in the data. Age is measured from the completed or scheduled date where one exists.
